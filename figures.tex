\subsection*{External Replication on the All of Us Research Program Cohort}

To assess generalizability beyond the training distribution, the BEHRT-LoRA model trained on MarketScan was evaluated without retraining on a held-out validation set drawn from the All of Us Research Program ($N = 50{,}000$). The All of Us cohort differs from MarketScan in its recruitment strategy, demographic composition, and data collection infrastructure, making it a stringent test of cross-cohort transferability. The four risk classes are: Class~0 ($>$10 years to fracture), Class~1 (1--5 years), Class~2 ($<$1 year), and Class~3 (5--10 years). Class distribution in the All of Us sample closely mirrors MarketScan (Class~0: 99.25\%; Class~1: 0.37\%; Class~2: 0.13\%; Class~3: 0.26\%), reflecting the rarity of near-term hip fracture in the general population. Bootstrapped confidence intervals are based on $N=1{,}000$ resampling iterations. Overall accuracy and ROC-AUC remain comparable to MarketScan results (Tables~\ref{tab:aou_overall}--\ref{tab:aou_roc_auc}), while minority-class precision is lower, consistent with the distribution shift between cohorts.
